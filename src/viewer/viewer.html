How to read the chart. Lower-left is better (smaller error at finer mesh). The amber spectral curve plunges with a steep slope — the eigenvalues converge fast. The amber pointwise curve only falls like h¹, and both coral (irregular-mesh) curves flatten or rise: strong consistency is not recovered, exactly as the Wardetzky–Mathur–Kälberer–Grinspun “no free lunch” theorem predicts. The takeaway for the manuscript: keep the spectral/variational O(h²) claim, retire the pointwise one.
